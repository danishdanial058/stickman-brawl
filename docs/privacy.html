Stickman Brawl: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Stickman Brawl is a free browser and Android game. This page explains what information the game uses and why. In short: the game does not ask for your real name, email or location, and the online server does not store anything about you.

What is saved, and where

  • Your progress (level, coins, armor, quests, settings and the player name you choose) is saved on your own device, in your browser or the app.
  • When you play on CrazyGames while logged in, your progress is also saved to your CrazyGames account through the CrazyGames SDK, so it follows you between devices. CrazyGames handles that data under its own privacy policy.

Online matches

  • When you tap Online, the game connects to our match server (run on Cloudflare, with Render as a backup) and sends your player name (or CrazyGames username), level, fighter and armor to the other player, so they can see who they are fighting. During the match it sends your button presses and the fight's positions.
  • The server only passes these messages between the two players. It does not store names, matches or results.
  • Like any internet service, our hosting providers (Cloudflare and Render) may briefly keep technical logs such as IP addresses to keep the service running and secure.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, passwords, email addresses or payment details are collected by the game.
  • No chat or user-generated content.
  • We do not sell or share your information with anyone.

Ads

On CrazyGames, video ads may play between matches. They are provided by CrazyGames, and their privacy policy covers them. The game itself does not show other ads or track you.

Children

The game is made for players aged 13 and over.

Deleting your data

To remove your progress, clear the game's data in your browser or app settings, or uninstall the app. CrazyGames account data can be managed through your CrazyGames account.
