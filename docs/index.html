      Special
      • Scribble Chain → Rising Quill
        Punch three times in a row. The third blow is Rising Quill, an uppercut that launches the enemy into the air.
      • Meteor Drop
        Kick in the air. You crash down at an angle trailing fire and crack the floor. Bounces you off on a hit.
      • Smudge Step
        Double-tap left or right to roll through attacks without taking damage. Costs stamina.
      • Sky Step
        Press up again in the air for a second jump. The Double Jump power-up makes it unlimited.
      • Specials
        Fill the yellow meter by hitting and getting hit, then press Special. Each fighter has their own: Headband's Inkblade Rush dashes through the enemy, Ninja's Shadow Step strikes from behind and can't be blocked, Robo's Rocket Fist flies across the stage and drags the enemy in, Champion's Crown Crush leaps and sends out shockwaves, Phantom's Haunt summons a ghost ally.
      • Fighters
        Headband is balanced. Ninja is fast but has 85 HP. Robo has 120 HP and is hard to knock away, but slower. Champion hits 15% harder. Phantom fades out while dodging and jumps higher in the air.
      • Ink Tempest
        Special while holding an Ultimate Orb: the sky goes dark and lightning hits every enemy on screen.
      • Stage hazards
        Fall off an edge or into a pit and you get a Ring Out: 25 damage and you drop back in. Lava burns, spikes launch you, belts drag you and ice is slippery. Hold down on a ledge to drop through it.
      • Block
        Cuts damage by 70%, but only from the front. Too many blocked hits break your guard.
      • Big Ink
        The boss uses Abyss Hammer, Blot Quake and Midnight Stampede. Jump the quake waves, roll through the stampede, and punish after a hammer.

      Weapons drop into the arena. Walk over one to grab it, punch to use it, press down to throw it. They wear out after a few hits.
