Moves

  • Punch, punch, punch
    The third punch in a row is an uppercut that launches the enemy into the air.
  • Kick in the air
    Dive kick. Jump high, then kick to slam down at an angle. Bounces you off on a hit.
  • Dodge roll
    Roll through attacks without taking damage. Costs stamina.
  • Block
    Cuts damage by 70%, but only from the front. Too many blocked hits break your guard.
  • Special
    Fill the yellow meter by hitting and getting hit, then dash strike. With an Ultimate Orb it hits every enemy on screen.
  • Big Ink
    The boss shrugs off punches. Jump his shockwaves, roll through his charge, and punish after a slam.
